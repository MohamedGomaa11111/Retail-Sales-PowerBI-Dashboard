# 📊 Retail & Executive Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An end-to-end interactive Business Intelligence solution built using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to analyze sales dynamics, channel performance, customer demographics, and geographical distribution.

## 🖼️ Dashboard Preview

![Sales Performance Dashboard](dashboard_preview.png)

## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Highlights

- **Channel Dynamics:** Deep-dive analysis comparing Online vs Store sales performance.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Breakdown of purchasing behaviors across Customer Types (Loyal, Retail, New) and Gender distribution.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Tracking feedback distribution across quality tiers.
- **Geospatial & Regional Coverage:** Interactive mapping tracking sales volumes and regional spread across key cities.
- **Product & Category Performance:** Identification of top-performing items to optimize inventory.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Applied

- **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL pipeline)
- **Analytics & DAX:** Measures for revenue aggregation and KPI tracking
